The craft

How a mandi comes together.

Mandi began in Yemen, and the method is simple and patient. This is how it is traditionally made.

  1. Season

    Meat or chicken is rubbed with a dry spice blend and left to take on flavour.

  2. Seal

    It cooks in a closed oven, so heat, smoke and steam work on it slowly.

  3. Catch

    Long-grain rice sits beneath and drinks up the juices as they fall.

  4. Share

    Everything lands on one big tray, meant to be eaten together.

Traditional method. Ask the team how your order is prepared.
